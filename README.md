# Aisle Up

A virtual shop that helps young people (16–24) in the UK get their first retail job. Shelby, the shop's guide, walks visitors through six aisles:

1. **Welcome to Retail**: what shop work is like and the types of shop
2. **Jobs on the Shelf**: common roles, hours and approximate pay
3. **The Application Counter**: filling in online applications, with weak vs strong answers
4. **Behind the Staff Door**: assessments, interviews, trial shifts and what happens after an offer
5. **The Fitting Room**: what to wear, the STAR method and calming nerves
6. **The Practice Till**: ten mock interview questions with feedback

Then **Checkout** links to official UK careers and job sites.

The whole site is one file, `index.html`, with no build step. Practice answers and checklist ticks are saved only in the visitor's own browser.
